Game Play Instructions

Mouse Look around
Wheel Zoom; past ~3× you raise the binoculars
Right click / B Snap binoculars up or down
1 / 2 Choose your call: 1 long cow call (carries far, for a bull you can’t see) · 2 short coaxing call (for a bull you can see)
Space Make the call: one of your recordings of that type plays start to finish
O Sound levels: your calls, moose, birds & wildlife, wind, forest, effects
V Water pour (a cow urinating, a classic close-in trick)
H / G Open or close these instructions / spotter debug readout
Esc Pause menu: change stand, settings, contact, exit

Reach a far bull with long calls. When he stops on his way in, a coaxing call gets him moving again. Call again within 30 seconds and you’re calling too often. Inside 75 yards, any call or pour gives you away. Keep the wind in your face: the dial is bottom left.
